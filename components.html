03 — Buttons

Buttons

Sized to their content, never stretched full-width by default. Primary for the one most important action; Secondary for a supporting action; Ghost for low emphasis.

04 — Chips

Chips

Rounded-rectangle, never a pill. Used for filters and multi-select tags — Surface at rest, solid Ink Navy when active.

05 — Batch stairs (signature)

Staircase as structure

The one place the branding guide's staircase motif becomes real structure instead of illustration: batch order is a genuine sequence, so the chips sit at five different heights, ascending left to right. Desktop/tablet only — narrower widths wrap the row and drop the stagger rather than let steps collide.

06 — Inputs

Input fields

Cream background distinguishes an editable field from a static Surface card. Gold border on focus.

07 — Checkboxes & Radios

Selection controls

Square for checkboxes, circular for radios — deliberately distinct shapes so the two are never confused at a glance.

08 — Status badges

Badges

Saturated text on a pale tint — never a solid saturated fill — so status stays legible without competing with Gold's role as the interactive accent.
