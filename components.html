Harbor & Amber

03 — Buttons

Buttons

Sized to their content, never stretched full-width by default. Primary for the one most important action; Secondary for a supporting action; Ghost for low emphasis.

04 — Chips

Chips

Rounded-rectangle, never a pill. Used for filters and multi-select tags — Surface at rest, solid Harbor Ink when active.

05 — Inputs

Input fields

Paper background distinguishes an editable field from a static Surface card. Amber border on focus.

06 — Checkboxes & Radios

Selection controls

Square for checkboxes, circular for radios — deliberately distinct shapes so the two are never confused at a glance.

07 — Status badges

Badges

Saturated text on a pale tint — never a solid saturated fill — so status stays legible without competing with Amber's role as the interactive accent.
